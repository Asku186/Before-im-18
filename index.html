How saving works

This site saves your progress in localStorage in the browser you are using. It normally stays there when you refresh or reopen the website on the same browser and device. It does not automatically sync to another phone or computer. Clearing site data, browser storage, or some private-browsing sessions can erase it. Export JSON backups regularly, keep them in a safe folder or cloud drive, and import one on another device when needed.

Important: GitHub Pages hosts the website files; it does not host or sync your private journal data.

Safety, always

Adventures should stay lawful and age-appropriate. “Sneak into a place” means a public place or event where entry is allowed, or somewhere you have permission to enter—never trespass or bypass security. Night swimming requires a designated safe location and responsible supervision; never swim alone at night. Overnight plans need a permitted location and appropriate adult support. Solo travel must follow age, transport, accommodation, and parental-permission rules. E-scooter rides must follow local age, helmet, and traffic laws. If an activity does not feel safe, adapt it or wait.
